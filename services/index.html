Purchase tracking, repaired

$300

Three working days. For stores below Plus that had tracking in Additional Scripts.

26 August 2026 was Shopify’s deadline for stores below Plus to upgrade their Thank you and Order status pages. Stores that hadn’t were upgraded automatically, and the new pages don’t run Additional Scripts, so anything that lived there (Google Analytics, the Meta pixel, an affiliate or conversion snippet) stopped running. I move Google Analytics, Meta, TikTok and one affiliate or conversion snippet to where Shopify now expects them, and check them with a real order.

Questions

How do you get into my store?

I send a collaborator request. You approve it in your Shopify admin and choose what it covers, so I see only what the job needs. No password changes hands, it doesn’t use one of your staff accounts, and you can remove it whenever you like. The speed report needs no access at all.

Will my live store change while you work?

No. Theme work happens on a duplicate of your theme. You preview it on your own products and publish when you’re ready. Tracking and product data live in your store’s settings rather than the theme, so for those I tell you exactly what I’ll change before I change it.

What if it can’t be done as described?

I tell you what I found and refund you in full that day. You don’t pay for a diagnosis.

Do you guarantee a PageSpeed score?

No. Lab scores move from one run to the next, and apps can add weight after I’ve finished. I guarantee that each cause the report lists passes its written check, with before and after reports you can check yourself. Where Google has data from your real shoppers, I show you that too.

My theme is old, or heavily customized.

Shopify’s older “vintage” themes, and themes carrying a lot of custom code, I quote rather than sell at these prices. Email me your store’s address and I’ll tell you which yours is.

Do we need a call?

No. Email is enough for everything on this page, and a written scope is clearer than a call. If you’d rather talk something through, say so.

Who does the work?

I do. No agency, no subcontractors. I’ve been building websites and Shopify stores for more than ten years, and I built and run my own Shopify store, so I’ve sat on the merchant’s side of the admin too.

How do I pay, and do I get a receipt?

By card through Stripe, from the button on each offer or from a link I email you. Stripe emails the receipt.

Can it be ready before Black Friday?

There’s no rush option and no rush fee: the working days listed are what careful work takes. The usual advice is to stop changing your theme about a week before Black Friday, so for anything that needs to be live by then, early November is the practical cut-off. After that, I’ll suggest we schedule it for December.

What happens after you deliver?

You get plain-English notes on what changed. Ask for any adjustment within 7 days; after that, the job is complete. For 14 days after delivery, anything my change broke is fixed free, and anything new gets its own fixed price.
