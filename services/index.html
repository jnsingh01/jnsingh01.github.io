Questions

How do you get into my store?

When the work needs access, I send a collaborator request. You approve it in your Shopify admin and choose what it covers, so I see only what the job needs. No password changes hands, it doesn’t use one of your staff accounts, and you can remove it whenever you like.

Will my live store change while you work?

Not from theme work: that happens on a duplicate of your theme, which you preview on your own products and publish when you’re ready. Tracking and product data live in your store’s settings and catalog, which your live store shares, so a change there does reach it. I show you exactly what will change, and apply it only after you approve it.

What if it can’t be done as described?

I tell you what I found, and you get a full refund. You don’t pay for a diagnosis.

Do you guarantee a PageSpeed score?

No. Lab scores move from one run to the next, and apps can add weight after I’ve finished. I guarantee that each cause the report lists passes its written check, with before and after reports you can check yourself. Where Google has data from your real shoppers, I show you that too.

My theme is old, or heavily customized.

Shopify’s older “vintage” themes, and themes carrying a lot of custom code, I quote rather than sell at these prices.

Do we need a call?

No. Messages on the platform we use are enough for everything on this page, and a written scope is clearer than a call.
