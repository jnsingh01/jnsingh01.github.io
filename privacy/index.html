Privacy

John Singh · what I collect, and what I don’t · updated 4 October 2026

The short version: this site doesn’t track you. If you buy something or email me, what you send is used only for the job, and you can ask me to delete it.

This website

  • No analytics, no cookies, no tracking scripts, and no forms that send anything to me.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. I don’t see those logs.
  • The speed check sends the address you type from your browser straight to Google PageSpeed Insights, which opens on Google’s own site, under Google’s privacy policy and cookies. This site doesn’t receive or store it.
  • The speed demo’s before and after pages load fonts from Google Fonts, and the before page loads scripts from cdnjs (Cloudflare), the way a real store would. Like any site you visit, those services see your browser’s request.
  • Stripe’s checkout, the demo store (hosted by Shopify) and the Google and Shopify pages I link to are those companies’ own sites, under their own privacy policies.

If you buy something or email me

  • What I get: your email address and the name and billing details you enter at Stripe’s checkout (such as country and postal code), your store’s address, and whatever you write to me.
  • Payments: Stripe processes them, under Stripe’s privacy policy. Your card details go to Stripe. I never see your full card number; Stripe shows me details such as the card’s brand, last four digits, expiry and country, and the results of its fraud checks.
  • Your store: during a job I have the collaborator access you approve, and I use it only for that job. I don’t copy your customers’ personal details out of your store. You can remove my access at any time from your Shopify admin.
  • What it’s used for: doing the job, replying to you, receipts and refunds, and tax records. It’s never sold, never used for advertising, and never added to a mailing list.
  • Who else handles it: the services I run the work on, such as Stripe for payments and Google for email, each under its own privacy policy.

How long I keep it

Our emails stay in my mailbox, and the job’s notes in my files, so I can answer questions, fixes, refunds or a card dispute about the work. I delete them when you ask, unless a dispute is still open. Payment records are kept as long as US tax rules require, and Stripe keeps its own records under its privacy policy.

Seeing or deleting your details

Email johnnassersingh@gmail.com to see what I hold about you, to correct it or to delete it. I reply within one working day. The only things I can’t delete are what tax rules say I must keep, what Stripe holds under its own policy, and, while a card dispute is open, the records it needs.

Changes
