    What the check counts

    No photo
    The product has no image, so its page shows the theme’s placeholder. Google needs a product image to show an item in its shopping results, and shoppers rarely buy what they can’t see.
    No description
    The description has no text (it may hold only an image). That leaves search engines little beyond the title to match a search to, and when no search description is set, Shopify builds the page’s meta description from the product description, so these pages usually go out with none.
    Short description
    Fewer than 30 words: often a single line from a supplier. Enough to exist, rarely enough to answer what a shopper wants to know.
    A single photo
    Only one image. Shoppers look for more than one angle, the scale, or the product in use before they buy.
    Small main photo
    The first image is under 800 pixels wide. On a phone the main photo usually fills the screen’s width, 1,080 pixels or more on most phones, so a smaller one is stretched and looks soft exactly where most shoppers see it. Shopify suggests 2,048 by 2,048 pixels for square product photos.
